# Bulk actions

Status: **Partly built** · Section: Records, history, and messages · [All features](README.md)

## What is there

tabsearch.ts close-by-text with preview, main.ts "Switch off all shown" extensions, Dismiss all, src/ui/select.ts and worlds.ts (Worlds: Select mode with click, Shift+click range, Ctrl+A, Select all, Invert, Download selected), main.ts notification centre (tick, Shift+click range, Select all shown, Invert, Dismiss selected), tests/select.test.ts | History (tick edits to narrow the export; Select all shown, Invert) | , Activity (same tick boxes and export) | , Extensions (ticked ones narrow Export and Switch off) , deploy wizard Instances (start, stop or restart the ticked ones, with a report of what was not done), src/core/bin.ts and `/api/worlds/bin` (Move to bin for the ticked worlds behind the two-key confirmation, a report of what was skipped and why, Bin with Put back; nothing is deleted), tests/bin.test.ts

## What is missing

permanent delete (left out on purpose: the app never deletes a world); nothing else: the actions that can skip an item (the bin, switching extensions off, the deploy instances) report what was not done and why, and downloads, dismissals and exports have nothing to skip
