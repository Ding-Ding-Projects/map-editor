# Languages and tone · 語言同語氣

> English, Hong Kong Cantonese or both, with separate funny-level sliders.

> 英文、香港粵語，或者兩樣一齊，仲有各自獨立嘅搞笑程度滑桿。

## Languages

Choose English, 粵語, or EN + 粵 to show both together. The choice is saved in your browser.

揀 English、粵語，或者 EN + 粵 嚟兩樣一齊顯示。你嘅選擇會儲存喺你個瀏覽器度。

## Funny levels

English and Cantonese each have a funny level from 1 to 5. It changes the wording of messages only; numbers, facts and warnings are identical at every level.

英文同粵語各自有一個由 1 到 5 嘅搞笑程度。佢只會改訊息嘅用字；數字、事實同警告喺每一級都一模一樣。
