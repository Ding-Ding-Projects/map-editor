# Theme colours and the colour picker · 主題顏色同揀色器

> Pick any colour and the whole Material 3 theme follows it.
>
> 揀任何一隻顏色，成個 Material 3 主題都會跟住佢變。

Category: [Settings](README.md) · [Read on the website, with pictures](https://ding-ding-projects.github.io/map-editor/appearance/)

## Colours of each part and your own logo

Settings › Appearance › Colours of each part gives the header, the tab list, the map toolbar and the cards their own tone of the theme colour: primary, secondary, accent or plain. Use my own logo puts a small picture (PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF, up to 256 KiB) in the header instead of the built-in mark, in this browser.

「設定（Settings）› 外觀（Appearance）› 每個部分嘅顏色（Colours of each part）」可以畀頁首、分頁清單、地圖工具列同卡片各自用主題顏色嘅唔同色調：主要、次要、強調或者淨色。「用我自己嘅標誌（Use my own logo）」會喺呢個瀏覽器入面，將一張細圖（PNG、JPEG、WebP 或者 GIF，最大 256 KiB）放喺頁首，代替內置嘅標記。

## Locking a setting

Every switch in Settings has a small lock next to it. A locked switch cannot be changed there or from the command palette until you unlock it, which asks first. Locks are saved with your other settings and included in exports.

設定入面每個開關隔籬都有一個細鎖。鎖咗嘅開關，喺嗰度或者喺指令面板度都改唔到，直到你解鎖為止，而解鎖會問咗你先。鎖會同你其他設定一齊儲存，匯出嗰陣都會包埋。

## Locking screens and panels

Settings › Comfort and focus can also Lock panel layout (panels cannot be dragged, floated or closed), Keep every tab open (the Close buttons disappear) and Lock the Settings screen (everything can be read but nothing changed, here or from the palette, until you press Unlock).

「設定（Settings）› 舒適同專注（Comfort and focus）」仲可以「鎖住面板版面（Lock panel layout）」（面板唔可以拖、浮起或者閂）、「保持每個分頁開住（Keep every tab open）」（啲「關閉（Close）」按鈕會消失）同「鎖住設定畫面（Lock the Settings screen）」（樣樣都睇得，但係喺呢度或者喺指令面板度都改唔到，直到你撳「解鎖（Unlock）」為止）。

## Quiet hours

Settings › Sounds › Quiet hours keeps sounds and reading aloud silent between two times, for example overnight.

「設定（Settings）› 聲音（Sounds）› 安靜時段（Quiet hours）」會喺兩個時間之間令聲音同朗讀保持靜音，例如成晚。

## Comfort

Read messages aloud (Settings › Sounds) speaks every message with your device's own voices at the speed you pick. Dark theme on a schedule (Appearance) switches to dark between two times, even across midnight. Lock editing (Comfort and focus) lets you look around safely: tools, undo, rollback and extension edits are blocked on that device until you unlock it.

「朗讀訊息（Read messages aloud）」（設定 › 聲音）會用你部裝置自己嘅聲音，以你揀嘅速度讀出每一個訊息。「按時間表用深色主題（Dark theme on a schedule）」（外觀）會喺兩個時間之間轉做深色，就算跨過午夜都得。「鎖住編輯（Lock editing）」（舒適同專注）等你可以安心周圍睇：喺嗰部裝置上面，工具、復原、還原同擴充功能嘅編輯都會被擋住，直到你解鎖為止。

## Dim sum surprise

Now and then, when Map Editor starts after setup, a small card in the corner shows a dish from the public Hong Kong dim sum catalogue with its English and Chinese names, jyutping and a short description. It closes by itself after a few seconds and never blocks anything. Turn it off in Settings › Appearance, or call one up from the command palette. The catalogue is downloaded by the server and kept in the data folder, so it still works offline after the first time.

間中，喺設定完之後 Map Editor 啟動嗰陣，角落會有張細卡片，顯示公開嘅香港點心目錄入面一款點心，連埋佢嘅英文同中文名、粵拼同一段短短嘅描述。佢過幾秒就會自己閂，永遠唔會阻住任何嘢。可以喺「設定（Settings）› 外觀（Appearance）」度閂咗佢，又或者喺指令面板度叫一個出嚟。個目錄係由伺服器下載，再放喺資料夾度，所以第一次之後，離線都照樣用得。

## Sounds

Settings › Sounds turns short sound effects on or off and sets their volume. They play after an edit, an undo or restore, a new or uploaded world, and when something goes wrong, always together with the on-screen message. Turning them off silences them straight away.

「設定（Settings）› 聲音（Sounds）」可以開或者閂短音效，同埋設定佢哋嘅音量。音效會喺一次編輯、一次復原或者還原、一個新建或者上載嘅世界之後播放，出咗問題嗰陣都會播，而且永遠都會同畫面上面嘅訊息一齊出現。閂咗就會即刻靜音。

## What each sound means

There are six sounds, and Settings › Sounds lists them with a play button each. Done (two rising notes) follows an edit or a saved setting. Something new (three rising notes) follows a new or uploaded world, a passkey or an extension being added. Taken back (two falling notes) follows an undo, a restore from history or settings being put back. Level up (a four-note fanfare) plays when a wording file dropped on the window is accepted. Not accepted (a low double note) plays when a dropped file is refused. Something went wrong (two low notes) plays with an error message. Where they come from: the app makes every sound itself as it plays, from plain tones; no sound file is shipped or downloaded, so there is nothing to license.

一共有六個聲，設定嘅「聲音」會逐個列出嚟，每個都有播放掣。「搞掂」（兩粒音向上）係編輯或者儲存設定之後。「有新嘢」（三粒音向上）係加咗新世界或者上載咗世界、加咗通行密鑰或者擴充功能之後。「收返」（兩粒音向下）係復原、由歷史還原，或者設定還原之後。「升級」（四粒音嘅號角聲）係拖入視窗嘅用字檔收咗嗰陣。「唔收」（低音兩下）係拖入嚟嘅檔唔收嗰陣。「出咗事」（兩粒低音）會同錯誤訊息一齊出。來源：每個聲都係程式播嗰陣用純音自己整出嚟；冇附帶亦冇下載任何聲音檔，所以冇授權問題。

## Clearing a field

Every text box shows a small × once it has text in it. It empties that box only and puts the cursor back in it.

每個文字框入面一有字，就會顯示一個細細嘅 ×。佢只會清空嗰一個框，再將游標放返入去。

## Settings sections

Settings is split into Language and voice, Appearance, Comfort and focus, Backup and history, and Coming later. The search box at the top finds a setting in any section. Comfort and focus has Reduce motion and Large text.

設定分做「語言同聲音（Language and voice）」、「外觀（Appearance）」、「舒適同專注（Comfort and focus）」、「備份同歷史（Backup and history）」同「遲啲推出（Coming later）」。最頂嗰個搜尋框可以搵到任何一區入面嘅設定。「舒適同專注」有「減少動態效果（Reduce motion）」同「大字（Large text）」。

## Backup and history

Export settings saves a .json file; Import settings reads one back and ignores anything it does not recognise. Every change is listed in Settings history with the old and new value, and Restore puts the old value back as a new change.

「匯出設定（Export settings）」會儲存一個 .json 檔案；「匯入設定（Import settings）」會讀返一個入嚟，佢唔識嘅嘢就唔理。每一項改動都會連埋舊值同新值列喺「設定歷史（Settings history）」度，而「還原（Restore）」會以一項新改動嘅形式將舊值放返去。

## Theme colour

Settings, Theme colour regenerates every Material 3 colour role from the colour you pick, in light and dark. Reset returns to the default green.

「設定（Settings）、主題顏色（Theme colour）」會由你揀嗰隻顏色，重新產生每一個 Material 3 顏色角色，淺色同深色都有。「重設（Reset）」會返去預設嘅綠色。

## The colour picker

Drag in the field or use the arrow keys, set hue and opacity, or type any colour: a CSS name, HEX, HEX8, rgb(), hsl(), hsv(), hwb(), lab(), lch(), oklab(), oklch() or cmyk(). Every format is shown with a copy button. Colours outside what screens can show are clipped and the picker says so. The contrast against the background is shown with the WCAG threshold it passes.

喺個色域度拖或者用方向鍵，設定色相同不透明度，又或者打任何一隻顏色：CSS 名稱、HEX、HEX8、rgb()、hsl()、hsv()、hwb()、lab()、lch()、oklab()、oklch() 或者 cmyk()。每一種格式都會連埋一個複製按鈕顯示出嚟。超出螢幕顯示得到嘅顏色會被裁走，而揀色器會講畀你知。同背景之間嘅對比度，會連埋佢通過嘅 WCAG 門檻一齊顯示。

## Animated rainbow

The rainbow is a separate choice, not a colour value. Its speed is one global level. With reduced motion it stays on one hue.

彩虹係一個獨立嘅選擇，唔係一個顏色值。佢嘅速度係一個全域嘅級別。開咗減少動態效果嘅話，佢會停喺一個色相度。
