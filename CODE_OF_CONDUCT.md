# Code of conduct

Be kind and be useful. Assume good intent, explain your reasoning, and keep discussion about the work.

Not welcome here: personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory language, publishing someone's private information,
or deliberately derailing a discussion.

If something is wrong, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's Security tab (Report a
vulnerability works for conduct reports too). Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and may block people who keep
breaking these rules.
